Home/Privacy Policy

At CableTvPros.com, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the steps we take to safeguard your data when you visit our website or use our services.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with CableTvPros.com, we may collect the following information:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, and other contact details.
  • Information you provide when requesting quotes, support, or service inquiries.
  • Device information, browser type, IP address, and website usage data.
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve website functionality and user experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information helps us provide a better experience by allowing us to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support.
  • Recommend TV, internet, and mobile service options based on your needs.
  • Improve our website performance, content, and overall user experience.
  • Send important service updates, promotional offers, and relevant communications (only where permitted).
  • Meet legal and regulatory requirements.

3. Information Sharing

CableTvPros.com does not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information with trusted service providers or business partners only when necessary to deliver requested services, process inquiries, or comply with legal obligations. All such partners are required to maintain the confidentiality and security of your information.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. While no online system is completely secure, we continuously work to maintain the highest level of data protection.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience, remember your preferences, analyze website traffic, and improve our services. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings, although some website features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, update, correct, or request the deletion of your personal information. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.

7. Third-Party Links

Our website may include links to third-party websites or service providers. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

8. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our business practices, legal requirements, or website functionality. Any updates will be posted on this page along with the revised effective date. Continued use of our website indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us:

  • Website: CableTvPros.com
  • Email: support@CableTvPros.com
